Engineer the environment in which the agent works: make project knowledge accessible, make
outcomes observable, and enforce architectural constraints through tools and checks. Humans
specify intent and improve the system's ability to carry it out.

**Apply it:** when a task repeatedly fails, identify the missing observation, action, constraint,
or feedback signal. Add that capability and evaluate whether the failure disappears.

**Scope:** the article describes an internal software-engineering experiment. Its throughput
and review practices are not default policies for other domains with expensive mistakes.
